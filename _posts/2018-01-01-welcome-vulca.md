Today we welcomed two visitors from the European makerspace movement [VULCA](https://vulca.eu/), Alexandre (on the left) and his colleague Clemence (second from the right).

![The T-Exchange meets VULCA](/images/the-vulca-visit-270119.jpg)\
_T-Exchange meets VULCA_

The visitors met with our trustees and filled us in how makerspaces were growing across Europe. The Vulca Makers Mobility Program had over 100 volunteers from European makers pace who had collectively visited over 250 makerspaces in Europe. We were informed about an earlier European Commission study that had visited 28 countries and generated a report [Available here](https://t.co/H1bduCntW4).
