On the 19th of July 2017 we were informed by OSCR (Scottish Charity Regulator) that our application for charitable status had been accepted. Consequently, The T-Exchange has become a legal entity in the form of a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation (SCIO).

Our registered name is _The T-Exchange_. NB The definite article, "The", is a formal part of the name.


Our charity number is **SC047588**.
