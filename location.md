We meet in Findhorn at the Findhorn Village Centre, which is NOT at the centre of the village but close to the coast road leading to the village centre. On the coast road, at the start of the one way traffic system, there is a right fork. This is shown in green on the map below and from the fork it is a very short distance to the Findhorn Village Centre.

We meet in the Garden Room at the rear of the Centre, which is usually accessed through the main entrance at the front of the building and indicated here by the lowest red dot.
